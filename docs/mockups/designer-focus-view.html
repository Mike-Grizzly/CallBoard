Act I

Scene 2 — A rocky seashore on the coast of Cornwall

(FREDERIC is discovered sitting in a despondent attitude at the base of a rock.)

FREDERIC.

Oh, take me back to the days when I was young and the world was full of pirates and pluck!

Yet, ere I leave this fatal spot, I owe a duty — and a duty must be done.

RUTH.

Oh, master, hear me ere you go —1

I, too, am a creature of the sea, and would not be left behind.

(Enter the PIRATE KING from the rocks above.)

FREDERIC.

Away! my duty calls me to the side of those who hunt your kind.

PIRATE KING.

For he is a pirate king! And it is, it is a glorious thing to be a pirate king!
