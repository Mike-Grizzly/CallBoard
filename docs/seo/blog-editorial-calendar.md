# Notes from the Prompt Desk: Blog Editorial Calendar

13-post roadmap for proscene.app/blog, derived from the SEO strategy (June 2026). Posts are ordered by publish priority. Every post follows the on-page standards in the strategy: primary keyword in the title tag (under 60 chars), meta description 120–155 chars, H2/H3s carrying secondary keywords, at least one docs link and one feature-page link, exactly one primary CTA, `Article` schema (plus `HowTo` on checklist/tutorial posts). Categories in Sanity: Walkthrough, SM craft, Product.

**Voice reminder:** written by people who have held the book. Real terminology, real constraints, no em-dashes, "Proscene" never "ProScene". One CTA per post, at most 1–2 inline product references.
