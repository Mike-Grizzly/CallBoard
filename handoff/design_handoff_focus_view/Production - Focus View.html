Act I — Scene 2 · A rocky seashore on the coast of Cornwall

(FREDERIC is discovered at the base of a rock, RUTH at the mouth of the cave.)

FREDERIC

Oh, take me back to the days when the world was full of pirates and pluck!

Yet ere I leave, I owe a duty — and a duty must be done.

RUTH

Oh, master, hear me ere you go —

I too am a creature of the sea, and would not be left behind.

(Enter the PIRATE KING from the rocks above.)

PIRATE KING

For I am a Pirate King! And it is, it is a glorious thing to be a Pirate King!

FREDERIC

Away! My duty calls me to the side of those who hunt your kind.
